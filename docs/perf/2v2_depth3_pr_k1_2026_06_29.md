# 2v2 Doubles — depth=3 measurement attempt with PR-K1 stack (2026-06-29)

Stack applied: PR-A + PR-B + PR-C + PR-D + PR-E + PR-F + PR-J + **PR-K1**. PR-K2 (continuous-HP fine bucketing) not in base for this run; it doesn't fire on these scenarios anyway.

Reproducer: `cargo run --release -p vgc-solver --example measure_2v2`

## Headline finding

**PR-K1 lands the dedup win Cole predicted.** Per-cell deduped outcomes drop from baseline's 1,344-2,112 to **3-4**. That's a **~350-700× collapse** at the frontier seam. The "~100 unique states" intuition was conservative — the real number is closer to 3-4 for clean attack/attack cells.

## §1 + §2 measured

| Scenario | Total cells | raw_combos/cell | outcomes/cell (baseline) | outcomes/cell (PR-K1) | Dedup ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| OHKO neutral | 32,256 | 3,072 | 1,344 | **4** | **336×** |
| Midgame 2HKO | 32,256 | 3,072 | 2,112 | **3** | **704×** |
| Switch-heavy | 32,256 | 3,072 | 2,112 | **3** | **704×** |

Per-cell wall-clock didn't drop (28ms → 25ms; still dominated by `step()` calls, not dedup). **But the outcome count is what matters for downstream recursion** — each outcome becomes a sub-tree node, so the per-ply branching factor crashes.

## §3 — depth-1/2/3 recursive solves

**Did not complete.** The example's mid-cell `wall_cap` patch (added during this measurement run) doesn't unblock the recursive solver — even with `wall_cap=240s` and `node_budget=100M`, the first run_one for OHKO-neutral d=1 hung past 15 minutes without printing a result. There's a deeper interaction between the mid-cell abort path and `double_oracle`'s convergence loop that needs separate investigation.

This is a **measurement-infrastructure bug**, not a perf finding. PR-K1's depth=2 wall-clock from the `tt_hit_rate` example (a different fixture and smaller doubles matrix) is reliable: **20s vs PR-J's 50s**, a 2.5× compound win.

## Projection for depth=3

Honest extrapolation, not measured:

- Pre-K1 per-ply branching: ~32k cells × ~2k outcomes = ~64M sub-tree nodes / ply
- Post-K1 per-ply branching: ~32k cells × ~3-4 outcomes = ~100k sub-tree nodes / ply
- **600× fewer sub-tree nodes per added ply**

Depth=3 estimate from PR-K1's depth=2 (20s on the tt_hit_rate fixture):
- Naïve: 20s × (100k / 2k) = **~17 minutes** for depth=3
- With TT compounding hits at deeper plies, likely **5-15 minutes**

This is the right ballpark to call **tractable for offline analysis**, but not interactive. For interactive depth=3, we'd need PR-K3 (counter/sub-HP bucketing) + likely PR-I.2 (action-independence) stacked on top of K1.

## Verdict

- **Depth=2 lossless 2v2 is now production-ready.** 20s wall-clock on a representative fixture.
- **Depth=3 lossless 2v2 is tractable for offline analysis**, projected 5-15 minutes per solve.
- **Depth=3 interactive isn't there yet** — needs another structural win on top.

## Recommended next perf PR

1. **PR-K3** (sub-HP `{0,>0}` + counter bucketing) — likely another 2-3× compound win on multi-turn-counter-heavy positions. Small implementation.
2. **Fix the §3 measurement bug** in `measure_2v2.rs` — the mid-cell wall_cap + double_oracle convergence issue. Worth doing before further perf claims so we can actually measure depth=3.
3. **PR-I.2** (action-independence tensor enumeration) — bigger lift, projected ~6-15× on neutral doubles turns.

PR-K3 is the next minimum-effort, maximum-leverage step.
